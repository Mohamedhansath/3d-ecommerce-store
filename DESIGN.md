# FORME design direction

Brand: FORME — an editorial storefront for footwear and everyday objects.

Voice: precise, calm, confident and human. Use short lines, concrete nouns and useful verbs. Avoid inflated luxury clichés, generic filler, fake reviews, fake scarcity and unsupported product claims.

Core copy:
- Hero: “Good things. Well formed.”
- Supporting line: “Footwear and everyday objects, chosen for how they feel, work and wear over time.”
- Collection: “Keep what works.”
- Approach: “Less to think about.”
- Journal: “Notice the little things.”

Palette: mineral paper #F1F2ED, product stage #E7EBE4, secondary surface #E5E9E2, graphite #202723, muted #747C75, border #D9DED7, forest accent #426B5A.

Typography: Space Grotesk for display; DM Sans for interface and body. Motion stays quiet; scroll progress is reserved for the 3D product turn.

Layout: product-first, generous whitespace, consistent content edges, compact product metadata, accessible touch targets. The 3D sneaker is generated locally with Three.js. Cart and wishlist are browser-local previews. Checkout is not active and does not process payments.
